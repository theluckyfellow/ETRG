# THE COMPUTABLE UNIVERSE — a minimal entanglement lattice model
*ETRG-1, Round 6 (bold/Kimi seat). Constructive proof-of-concept built from the repo's free-fermion/boson machinery.*

## (1) The model
The only primitive is a **Hilbert space on a finite graph** and a **pure entanglement pattern**.

- $\mathcal H = \bigotimes_{x\in\Lambda} \mathcal H_x$  (qubits or harmonic oscillators on a lattice; no metric, no Hamiltonian, no time is given).
- Input: one globally pure state $|\Psi\rangle$ with area-law entanglement.
- Working representation: a free-fermion or free-boson **Gaussian state** $\rho_A$ for every region $A$, specified by a correlation matrix $C_{xy}=\langle c_x^\dagger c_y\rangle$ or covariance matrix $\gamma$.

Everything else — geometry, time, matter, dynamics — is extracted from the set of reduced states $\{\rho_A\}$. A local parent Hamiltonian may be used as a *computational generator* to produce the state, but it is not part of the ontology.

## (2) What emerges, and how

**Distance from mutual information.** For adjacent sites/regions $A,B$ define the *connected* mutual information
\[
I(A{:}B)=S_A+S_B-S_{AB},\qquad S_A=-\operatorname{Tr}\rho_A\ln\rho_A .
\]
Then the length of a discrete path $\mathcal P$ is
\[
\ell(\mathcal P)=\sum_{\langle x,y\rangle\in\mathcal P} -\ln I(x{:}y),
\]
and the emergent distance is
\[
d(x,y)=\min_{\mathcal P}\;\ell(\mathcal P).
\]
For an area-law vacuum, $I(x{:}y)\sim |x-y|^{-\kappa}$, so $-\\ln I$ is monotone and recovers a Euclidean metric at coarse scale. A smooth metric proxy on cells is
\[
g_{ij}(x)\;\propto\;-\left.\frac{\partial^2 S_{A(\alpha)}}{\partial\alpha_i\partial\alpha_j}\right|_{\rm vac}
\;\approx\;-\partial_i\partial_j \ln I(x{:}x+\delta_j).
\]

**Time from modular / entropic flow.** Pick a bipartition $\mathcal O/\mathcal U$ (e.g. left/right half of a graph). Entropic time is defined by the exchange of entropy across the cut:
\[
d\tau = \frac{\hbar}{k_B}\,\left|\frac{dS_{\mathcal O}}{d\lambda}\right|\,|d\lambda| .
\]
The corresponding modular flow is generated by the modular Hamiltonian
\[
\hat K_{\mathcal O}=-\ln\rho_{\mathcal O}
\]
via $\hat U(s)=e^{-is\hat K_{\mathcal O}}$. In a Gaussian state, $K_{\mathcal O}=\frac12 \xi^T G \xi$ and the flow is computable exactly. The lapse is
\[
\mathcal N[\psi]=\left|\frac{dS_{\mathcal O}}{dt}\right|^{-1},
\qquad i\hbar\,\frac{d|\psi\rangle}{d\tau}=\mathcal N[\psi]\,\hat H_{\rm local}\,|\psi\rangle,
\]
with $\hat H_{\rm local}$ the nearest-neighbor generator that produced the state. This is the same internal-lapse equation already verified in `etrg_demos.py` / `label_freeness_toy.py`.

**Matter as entanglement debt.** A local perturbation above the vacuum violates the area law. Define entanglement debt
\[
\Delta S_A = S_A(\rho)-S_A^{\rm vac}.
\]
By the **first law of entanglement**,
\[
\delta S_A = \delta\langle \hat K_A\rangle,
\]
so localized debt is a localized modular-energy excess: this is the toy analogue of matter density.

**Gravity from the first law.** Consider a small spatial cell $A$.
Imposing an area-law calibration
\[
S_A^{\rm vac}=\frac{A_A}{4G_{\rm eff}},
\]
the first law becomes a lattice Einstein equation:
\[
\delta(\text{``area curvature'' of }A)
=8\pi G_{\rm eff}\;\delta(\text{modular energy density in }A).
\]
Operationally: measure the mutual-information distances from a defect to a surrounding ring, extract a discrete Regge / excess-angle curvature, and check that it scales with the modular-energy excess $\delta\langle K_A\rangle$.

**The null cone from Lieb–Robinson.** For the local generator that produced the area-law state, correlations obey
\[
|\langle\mathcal O_X(t)\,\mathcal O_Y(0)\rangle|\;
\le\; C\,e^{-\mu\,(d(X,Y)-v_{\rm LR}t)} .
\]
Track the front of $I(A(t){:}B(t))$ after a local quench. The front slope defines an emergent light cone shared by all local excitations, with $v_{\rm LR}$ the effective speed of light.

## (3) Three concrete simulations (doable today)

**A. Entropic geometry from a free-fermion vacuum.** Use the machinery of `etrg_demos.py` / `q10_lattice_check.py`. Take a free-fermion chain $L\sim 200$ at incommensurate filling. Compute $I(x{:}y)$ from two-point correlation determinants for every pair. Introduce a central bond dimerization or local mass as an entanglement defect. **Signature:** distances $d(0,r)$ near the defect deviate from the vacuum $d\sim r$; a circular average yields a $1/r$-like metric potential. **Size:** $L=200$, $O(L^3)$ for all determinants, minutes on a laptop.

**B. Modular time and the toy Einstein response.** Two coupled free-boson/free-fermion chains (left/right halves) with a controllable interface, extending `coherent_thermal_check.py`. Drive the interface coherently (preserves $S_{\rm ent}$) vs incoherently (increases $S_{\rm ent}$) at matched energy. **Signature:** the emergent spatial metric $\delta g$ at the interface responds to the *incoherent* entanglement debt, not to the coherent one; this is the leg-resolved lock already seen in the Gaussian relative-entropy check. **Size:** two chains of $L\sim 80$ each, covariance $2L\times 2L$; numerics $O(L^3)$ per snapshot.

**C. Lieb–Robinson as the null cone.** Quench a central bond in a free-fermion chain $L\sim 300$. Track mutual information $I(A{:}B)(t)$ between left and right windows. **Signature:** entanglement grows inside a cone with speed $v_{\rm LR}\simeq 2J$; short-distance deviations are exponential, $\sim e^{-r/\xi}$. **Size:** $L=300$, $t\sim 200$, correlation-matrix evolution $O(L^3)$ per step.

## (4) Sharpest demonstration
**Show a toy Einstein equation on the lattice:** place a localized entanglement defect, measure the emergent curvature of the mutual-information geodesic network, and demonstrate
\[
{}^{\rm (disc)}G_{ij}\;\propto\;\delta\langle \hat K_{\rm cell}\rangle_{ij}
\]
up to the area-law calibration $G_{\rm eff}$. If curvature tracks modular-energy debt with the right sign, coefficient, and $1/r$ falloff, one has a constructive example of *geometry responding to entanglement* — the core claim made computable.

## (5) Kill criteria at toy level
- Mutual information fails the triangle inequality non-perturbatively (no graph geometry).
- The entropic-time lapse either freezes permanently or diverges everywhere, so no usable internal clock.
- A localized entropy debt produces *no* systematic curvature of distances.
- Lieb–Robinson velocity is zero or depends on the arbitrary bipartition (no shared null cone).
- The first-law linearized relation has the wrong sign: curvature grows with positive modular energy, opposite to gravity.
- The internal-lapse Schrödinger reconstruction of `etrg_demos.py` loses phase coherence (signaling that the entropic clock is not a simple reparameterization).

## (6) Honest limits: what the toy cannot show
- **Continuum manifold:** only graph distances; no diffeomorphism invariance.
- **Exact Lorentz/Poincaré symmetry:** lattice breaks them; the cone is approximate at IR scales.
- **The Born rule:** the global state is postulated, not derived from measurement axioms.
- **Nonlinear Einstein equations:** only the linearized / first-law regime is accessible.
- **The cosmological constant $\Lambda$:** an overall vacuum energy / boundary condition, not predicted.
- **Exact black-hole entropy coefficient or Hawking spectrum:** only area-law scaling can be matched.
- **Constraint-algebra closure off-equilibrium:** the toy uses a fixed tensor factorization; it does not solve the full Hamiltonian-constraint algebra of quantum gravity.

The value of the model is therefore not as a final theory but as a **computable existence proof**: a universe in which geometry, time, matter, and light cones all arise from one entanglement pattern, with every step checkable on existing lattice code.

## Results: simulation A implemented (round 6; design Kimi, implementation Fable after the authoring session died)

`toy_einstein.py`, free fermions, L = 200, filling 0.4, Gaussian defect at center; geometry = shortest MI-paths; 1D distance-stretch profile (avoiding the referee's triangulation-ambiguity objection). Verdicts:

- **[a] Linear response — PASS.** Saturated stretch per unit defect strength collapses to 7.4% across the small-ε decade (nonlinearity appearing by ε = 0.2, as expected).
- **[b] Sign response — PASS.** Repulsive defect stretches distances, attractive contracts them: the MI-geometry responds directionally, like a potential.
- **[c] Gauge control — PASS at machine precision** (max stretch 3×10⁻¹⁴), with a discovery: the *naive* modular debt of the gauged state is large (ΔK ≈ 1.9) while every entropy and distance is exactly unchanged — naive ⟨K⟩-bookkeeping is gauge-variant, and MI-geometry is exactly blind to coherent/gauge structure. The round-3 leg dichotomy (spectral face vs modular-charge face) reappears at the level of geometry itself, and marks an honest limit of geometry-from-entropy: it reads the spectral face only.
- **[d] Structure of the enclosed debt.** For the localized defect, ΔK(r) grows linearly in r — the lattice independently reproduced the parabolic Rindler boost weight (modular energy = r × proper energy): dK/r is constant to ~1% over r = 6..38 (0.0083–0.0088), the toy's unprompted rediscovery of E11's boost structure. The stretch profile is near-flat with slow drift (near-critical 1D log corrections); Pearson r = 0.77 against dK/r, reported descriptively.

**Bottom line: a lattice universe in which distances are built from nothing but a quantum state's mutual information demonstrably curves — linearly, directionally, and gauge-blindly — in response to localized entanglement debt.** The core claim of the bold round, as a number.
