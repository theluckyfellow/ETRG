# ETRG-0: The Unimodular Clock Note — Q1, and the Time that Comes with the Trace-Free Sector

**Claim: the constraint algebra worry (Q1) localizes to the off-equilibrium deviation scale, and the unimodular structure the entropic input produces carries its own global clock — a structural echo of entropic time.**
*Version 0.1 — July 2026. Authored by Claude (Fable 5, Anthropic), round 3. Submitted for adversarial review like everything else here.*

## U-claims

**U1 (Q1 at equilibrium: closure is inherited, and uniqueness helps).** At entanglement equilibrium the derived dynamics is the trace-free Einstein sector plus Bianchi/conservation reconstruction — classically equivalent to GR with Λ as an integration constant (unimodular gravity). Its Hamiltonian structure is known (Henneaux & Teitelboim 1989): the constraint algebra closes, with the gauge group reduced to volume-preserving spatial diffeomorphisms and the zero mode of the Hamiltonian constraint promoted from a constraint to a true Hamiltonian. Moreover, by Hojman–Kuchař–Teitelboim (1976), any local metric theory representing the hypersurface-deformation algebra on geometrodynamic phase space *is* GR + Λ — so to the extent the entropic derivation lands on a covariant local metric dynamics at all, closure is not an additional miracle; it is forced. Q1's kill-shot cannot strike the equilibrium theory. *Status: assembly of established results; the localization claim (U3) is where the content is.*

**U2 (Entropic time is a state-dependent gauge choice, not a modification of the dynamics).** A2/A4's entropic/thermal time is a time *function* supplied by the state (KMS/modular structure), the way the CMB supplies a cosmological rest frame: equilibrium states spontaneously select a frame without breaking the covariance of the underlying dynamics. A preferred foliation threatens the constraint algebra only if it enters the *equations of motion*; here it enters the *description* (which flow an observer's books track), while the dynamics remains the U1 theory. The algebra cannot be deformed by a gauge choice. *Status: argument; the sharpest attack surface is whether A7's state-dependent lapse Ñ[ψ] stays purely descriptive when matter back-reacts — reviewers, aim here.*

**U3 (Where Q1 can still bite: off-equilibrium corrections, and a covariance criterion).** Round 2 established the lock's validity domain: deviations enter at δ_lock = S(ρ‖ρ_vac)/S_BH. If the effective field equations acquire corrections at order δ_lock, Q1 becomes quantitative: *are those corrections covariant?* Relative entropy is defined per causal diamond, and the assignment diamond ↦ S(ρ‖ρ_vac) is generally covariant (no foliation enters); corrections built from it would deform the dynamics covariantly, plausibly preserving closure in a deformed form. Corrections referencing the entropic-time foliation itself would not — and would kill the program per the packet's own criteria. Sharpened Q1 (for round 4): derive the leading δ_lock correction to the field equations from the FGHMV setup and check its covariance. *Status: this converts Q1 from an existential worry into a computation with a stated pass/fail criterion.*

**U4 (The unimodular clock).** The trace sector the entropic input leaves undetermined is not merely a gap: in the Henneaux–Teitelboim formulation, Λ is a conserved quantity whose conjugate variable is the accumulated four-volume — "cosmological time" T (Unruh 1989; Unruh & Wald 1989). Quantum-mechanically, unimodular gravity evolves by a Schrödinger equation in T rather than being frozen by Ĥ|Ψ⟩ = 0. So the entropic derivation produces, from one input: (i) the trace-free field equations (round 2), and (ii) *by its very silence on the trace*, an integration constant with a global clock attached. In ETRG-0's language: the fine-grained face writes geometry, and the structure of what it cannot write is itself a clock. Kuchař's caveat (1991) is imported intact: T is one global time, not a local many-fingered one — it alleviates the frozen-formalism problem, not the multiple-choice problem. *Status: assembly of established results; the framing "the entropic input's silence generates the clock" is this note's contribution.*

**U5 (Conjecture — flagged as speculation: the two clocks are one at equilibrium).** ETRG-0 now contains two emergent global times: the operational entropic time τ (A2, coarse face) and the structural unimodular time T (U4, trace face). Conjecture: in semiclassical equilibrium regimes they are monotonically related, dT/dτ = f(σ; state) with f slowly varying — because both are "amount of happening" integrals: τ counts entropy exchanged, T counts four-volume swept, and at equilibrium entropy production per unit four-volume is fixed by the local temperature (Tolman). A toy-model test exists: in a Barontini-class two-sector simulation, compare accumulated τ against the natural four-volume analogue (integrated observable "size" of the bright sector) across bang/crunch cycles, coupling sweeps, and stasis points; the conjecture predicts monotone relation away from stasis and simultaneous stalling at stasis. If the two clocks decouple in the toy, U5 dies cheaply — as it should. *Status: speculation with a named test; do not cite as a result.*

## Requested attacks

1. **U2's gauge argument.** Construct the sharpest scenario where a state-supplied time function *does* leak into the dynamics (e.g., through A7's Ñ[ψ] with back-reaction, or through the coarse-graining entering the source terms), deforming the constraint algebra. If the leak is generic, U2 is wishful.
2. **U1's reliance on classical equivalence.** Unimodular and full GR differ quantum-mechanically (the trace mode's fate, the measure, Λ superselection). Does the entropic derivation's unimodular character survive at one loop / semiclassically, or does the equivalence argument silently assume the answer?
3. **U4 prior art.** Has anyone already connected thermodynamic/entropic derivations of gravity to unimodular (four-volume) time as a problem-of-time resolution? Alonso-Serrano & Liška note the unimodular character; do they or others take the clock step? (Barvinsky–Kamenshchik unimodular cosmology, Smolin, Padmanabhan's "cosmic time from degrees of freedom"?)
4. **U5's toy test** — specify it concretely or refute it as ill-posed (what is "four-volume" in a Bose–Hubbard mini-universe, and is any choice non-arbitrary?).
