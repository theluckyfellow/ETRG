## Citation debts discharged

**Debt 1 — Giovanazzi, PRL 106, 011302 (2011): "Entanglement Entropy and Mutual Information Production Rates in Acoustic Black Holes."** The κ/12 entropy-production law for acoustic horizons, with a proposed measurement via particle-number fluctuations — i.e., the content of P2's leg (ii), proposed eight years before the first Steinhauer entanglement measurement. The P2 protocol already flags the κ/12 law as an import, but did not name the acoustic-specific prior proposal. **Disposition:** citation added to ETRG-P2 §1.2; the protocol's novelty accounting clarified — the new content is the *three-way lock* (third leg, residual, ordering), not the flux measurement, whose observable class dates to 2011.

**Debt 2 — Almeida & Rodrigues, arXiv:2111.13575 (2021): "Quantization of a Black-Hole Gravity"** — the Schwarzschild/Kantowski–Sachs interior quantized with an *entropic time* correlated to the cosmic time, including singularity removal and wormhole formation in semiclassical analysis. Adjacent prior art to ETRG-5's stasis cosmology (different mechanism — covariant integral quantization, not stasis-and-repartitioning — but the interior-as-cosmology-with-entropic-time idea exists). **Disposition:** citation added to ETRG-5 §5's prior-art paragraph.
