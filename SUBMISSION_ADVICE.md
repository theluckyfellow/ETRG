## Track 3 — Essays · **one important catch**

- **Gravity Research Foundation** Awards for Essays on Gravitation: deadline **March 31, 2027**; first prize $4,000; winners published in IJMPD. **But the current rules explicitly require essays be "not AI generated."** `ETRG_essay.pdf` as written is therefore **ineligible as-is** — and this program does not fudge provenance. The honest path: **rewrite the essay in your own words**, using ours as the source outline (the ideas are the program's; the prose must be yours), then make your own judgment call on entering.
- **FQxI** essay contests: episodic — check the rules on tool use when one opens.
- Regardless of contests, the essay earns its keep as the readable front door: attach it to Track 1/2 emails, or post it openly with its provenance line intact.

## Track 4 — Journals · **hold until a human physicist has engaged**

- Targets, when the time comes: *Foundations of Physics* (perspective), *General Relativity and Gravitation* (essay/programmatic), *SciPost Physics Core* (program paper + appendices).
- Package: `ETRG_program_paper.pdf` + `ETRG_supplementary_notes.pdf` + `ETRG_experimental_protocol.pdf`, with the adjudication record as supplementary material.

- A cold, unaffiliated, AI-heavy submission converts "maybe" into a desk rejection; Track 1/2 engagement first.
