# ETRG-8: The Path to Theory — What It Would Take

*August 2026. Written by Claude (Fable 5) in answer to the originator's question: "What would it take to get this to physical-theory level?" The program's honest classification — a research program with a falsifiability agenda, not yet a physical theory — appears on every title page. This note names the gates between the two, ranked, and calibrates what "theory" actually requires. Sources: ETRG-7 §6 (the open problems, ranked), the referee record, and the standing kill criteria. Nothing here is new physics; it is the program's own gap analysis, consolidated.*

## 0. The calibration: what "theory" requires

General relativity is a physical theory **without** deriving the matter spectrum. So the hardest open problem on the program's list (ETRG-7 §6 item 5 — why these fields, these generations) is *not* required for theory status, however much every panelist wants it.

What GR has that ETRG lacks is exactly two things:

1. **Dynamical equations derived from its own principles**, valid away from special configurations — not imported at equilibrium.
2. **At least one confirmed prediction it did not inherit.** (For GR: light bending, before it was measured.)

ETRG currently reproduces GR's confirmations *by reproducing GR*; its derivational core is borrowed at equilibrium; and it is candid that it predicts no new weak-field numbers. Those two lacks define the bar. They resolve into three gates, in order.

## Gate 1 — Structural closure (live-or-die; pure theory work)

Two items, both already flagged in ETRG-7 §6 as where the program could internally break. Passing them yields "theory" in the **weak sense**: a closed, self-consistent formalism reproducing GR+QM in all tested regimes, with its own derivational ordering.

**1a. The continuum / Type III restatement.** Every lattice proof in this repository is regularized. Real wedge and diamond algebras are Type III₁, where the density matrices, entropies, and modular kernels the program manipulates do not strictly exist. The Q10 selection theorem and the factorization toehold must be restated in Tomita–Takesaki language — half-sided modular inclusions, the Connes cocycle. The mathematical technology now exists: the crossed-product constructions (Witten 2021; Chandrasekaran–Longo–Penington–Witten 2022) were built to render gravitational entropies well-defined (Type II) in exactly this setting, and the restatement should be built on them. This is a genuine research problem for an algebraic-QFT specialist — plausibly one to three years *if it works*.

**1b. The constraint algebra off-equilibrium.** The entanglement-first-law route to the (trace-free) Einstein equations is an equilibrium import (Jacobson; FGHMV; Alonso-Serrano & Liška). A theory needs the constraint algebra to close away from equilibrium; the non-conformal diamond-measure obstruction is precisely where it might not. This is the honest kill criterion doing its job: a proof that closure fails ends the program, and that is a legitimate outcome. A proof that it closes gives the program field equations of its own.

## Gate 2 — A number of its own

Without Gate 2, even a structurally closed ETRG is a *reformulation with a different explanatory ordering* — genuinely valuable (statistical mechanics stood in exactly that relation to thermodynamics before fluctuations were measured) but not a distinct physical theory. The candidates, in order of nearness:

- **The no-Page-knee prediction for sustained horizons** (ETRG-P2 §9). Already in hand, genuinely anti-naive, and self-endangering: a knee appearing kills the program by its own K-rows. The closest thing to an owned number today.
- **The cosmological lock's second observable** (ETRG-7 §6 item 4). One order-of-magnitude consistency (the de Sitter / MOND coincidence at factor 4.5) is not a lock; a second independent observable tied through the same modular rate would be. This is the program's top *analytic* task.
- **The σ calibration** — entropic time's absolute exchange rate, which P2 deliberately routes around with ratios. Three acceptable resolutions: fix it, explain it, or prove it conventional (as c is a unit choice in relativity). The first two could yield a measurable deviation somewhere; the third would at least close the question honestly.

## Gate 3 — Contact

Someone runs ETRG-P2, or a Barontini-class extension of it. Note the asymmetry: **passing the three-way lock does not confer theory status** — much of the lock is defensible from standard analog-gravity physics, and a violation would refute more than ETRG. But *failing* a leg-ordering row or a Δ_weld row kills the program by its own criteria, and the discriminator D and the Page-leg rows are the program's own content. A framework cannot be a theory without having put its own rows at risk at least once.

## 4. What would not be enough

More lattice toys. More routes on the unification graph. More internal referee cycles. That machinery has done its job — the claims are hardened, the dead ends are mapped, the suite reproduces bit-identically under third-party audit — and it is now at diminishing returns. The binding constraints all live in territory the repository itself says needs a human:

- Gate 1 needs a **mathematical physicist** (algebraic QFT).
- Gate 3 needs an **experimental group** (analogue gravity, cold atoms).
- Only Gate 2's middle item is homework the program can still do itself.

## 5. The sequence

1. **Put 1a and 1b in front of one qualified algebraic-QFT person.** The highest-leverage single act available to the program. ETRG-7 and the referee packet are the hand-off documents.
2. **Keep ETRG-P2 polished as the standing ask** of the cold-atom community; the arithmetic is independently verified and the campaign is costed.
3. **Work the second cosmological observable and the σ question internally** in the meantime — they are the only gates that do not require recruiting anyone.

Gate 1 is where the program lives or dies. Gate 2 is what would make it matter.
