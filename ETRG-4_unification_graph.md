# ETRG-4: The Unification Graph — Time, Entropy, Causality, Gravity

*August 2026, Kimi-K3, at the originator's reframed brief: "My hypothesis isn't important. I think time, entropy, causality, and gravity are all part of a unified system. Explore all ways that could be possible." This document maps the possibility space. ETRG-0 through ETRG-3 built one route up the mountain (Route A below) and, in the building, presented it as the mountain; the originator's own statement of the intuition — "entropy causes a shift in the spatial orientation of causality, and light, moving at the speed of causality, is more affected by that" — belongs to a different route (Route B), which this map develops for the first time in this repository. Every edge carries its best theorem or its honest flag. Nothing here is a claim of unification; it is a claim about where unification could live.*

---

## 1. The four vertices, and what "unify" can mean

**Time** (flow, rate, arrow) · **Entropy** (coarse-grained exchange; fine-grained entanglement) · **Causality** (the cone structure: what can influence what) · **Gravity** (the metric and its dynamics).

"Unify" has three strengths, and the routes below differ in which they claim:

- **U1 (kinematic interlock):** the four are different readings of one underlying structure; each is computable from the others.
- **U2 (dynamical interlock):** the dynamics of each is constrained by the others (Einstein's equation as a consistency condition, not a fundamental law).
- **U3 (identity):** there is one primitive; the four are emergent bookkeeping formats. (The strong claim; every route gestures at it, none has proven it.)

Time is the order of the cones, with its rate read from the entropic scale. This is the backbone of Route B.

These are the load-bearing nodes. Every route below is a different way of promoting one or more of them to *the* primitive.

## 4. The routes

### Route A — Entropy-first (the ETRG-0…3 route)
**Primitive:** entropy relative to a partition. Time = modular flow; geometry = entanglement structure; causality = inherited from the geometry; gravity = thermodynamic consistency of the ledger.

**Weakness:** the partition is a second, unexplained primitive (the hard kernel; rounds 7–9's toehold is the first traction); causality is derivative, which sits badly with the originator's intuition that it is *causality* that entropy acts upon.

Gravity = cones + Ω. Time = the order of the cones; clock *rate* = Ω's reading (the lapse is entropic, recovering A4). Dynamics: the cones and Ω must satisfy mutual consistency — Jacobson's argument is exactly the consistency condition (δQ = T dS on the cones ⇒ Einstein).

**Weakness:** Ω-from-entropy is asserted at horizons and diamonds, not derived in general; the conformal decomposition is kinematics — the *dynamics* still needs Jacobson's thermodynamic input; no lattice toy yet (see §6).

### Route C — Thermodynamics-first (Jacobson–Verlinde lineage)
**Primitive:** the Clausius relation on local horizons. Gravity = equation of state of the vacuum; time = thermal flow; causality = assumed local Rindler structure; entropy = horizon heat.
**Strength:** the shortest derivation of Einstein's equation from entropy; maximal U2 content.
**Weakness:** circularity risk (local Rindler horizons presuppose the metric); Verlinde's dissipative version is falsified by interferometry (ETRG-0 A6); says nothing about the matter spectrum.

### Route D — Holography-first (it-from-qubit; AdS/CFT, RT)
**Primitive:** a boundary quantum theory; bulk geometry = its entanglement structure; time and causality = boundary dynamics and its cone.
**Strength:** the only route with a proven-in-context full unification (AdS/CFT); RT is the sharpest entropy-geometry theorem.
**Weakness:** our universe is not AdS (the Λ embarrassment, inherited intact); the boundary theory for de Sitter is unknown; this is a unification of gravity with *a* quantum theory, not obviously with *ours*.

### Route E — Order-first (causal sets)
**Primitive:** a discrete partial order. Geometry = order + counting ("order + number = geometry"); entropy = counting; time = the order.
**Strength:** the most radical parsimony — the conformal-factor slot is filled by *counting*, which is what entropy is; discreteness cures the continuum divergences the lattice suite keeps fighting.
**Weakness:** dynamics (the Benincasa–Dowker action) is young; Lorentz invariance of a random order is a theorem only in a weak sense; contact with the entanglement machinery is undeveloped.

### Route F — Algebra-first (Connes–Rovelli–von Neumann)
**Primitive:** a von Neumann algebra with a state. Time = modular flow (thermal time hypothesis at full strength); causality = the algebra's inclusion structure (half-sided modular inclusions generate the Poincaré group — Wiesbrock, Borchers); entropy = relative entropy of states; geometry = to be derived.
**Strength:** the mathematically deepest; time and causality both fall out of modular theory; ETRG's Q10 and the round-7/9 lattice results are this route's lattice shadows.
**Weakness:** the circularity the round-6 referee convicted (the AQFT nets presuppose the spacetime they generate); gravity's dynamics not yet in scope.

## 4b. The routes the first draft missed (added at the originator's audit)

The six above bind the four vertices most directly, but the terrain is bigger. Seven more, in the same format, with the same honesty:

### Route G — Geometry-quantized-first (loop quantum gravity / spin foams)
**Primitive:** quantum states of geometry itself (spin networks; spin foam amplitudes). Gravity = the quantized metric; black-hole entropy = counting of horizon microstates (the LQG area spectrum gives S ∝ A with the Immirzi parameter fixing the coefficient); time = the scandal (the frozen formalism; the problem of time is LQG's open wound); causality = built into spin foams by hand.
**Strength:** the most developed *direct* quantization of GR; the horizon-entropy counting is a real theorem in context.
**Weakness:** time is absent at the fundamental level (exactly the problem ETRG's A2 exists to solve — the routes are complements, not rivals); the continuum/semiclassical limit is not fully controlled; matter is inserted, not explained.

### Route H — Null-ray-first (twistor theory)
**Primitive:** light rays (null geodesics) themselves; spacetime points are *derived* (as intersections of rays). Causality is literally the primitive object; gravity = deformed twistor space (the nonlinear graviton); entropy and time = peripheral.
**Strength:** Route B's closest living relative — the proof that a causality-first construction can *work* (in 4D, for self-dual gravity); scattering amplitudes' twistor miracles are real.
**Weakness:** the full (non-self-dual) gravitational problem resisted forty years; entropy has no natural twistor home; the route to quantum theory is via amplitudes, not spacetime.

### Route I — Change-first (shape dynamics / Barbour relationalism)
**Primitive:** the configuration space of shapes, with change (not time) as fundamental. Time = distinguished by the dynamics (ephemeris time; Janus points); the arrow = growth of shape-space complexity/entropy; gravity = conformal-geometrodynamics (best-matching); causality = inherited.
**Strength:** the most radical solution to the problem of time on the market; the arrow of time gets a *derivation* attempt (Janus-point entropy growth), which every other route assumes.
**Weakness:** best-matching is technically arduous; contact with quantum theory is partial; the entropy used is not obviously the entanglement entropy of Routes A/B.

### Route J — Triangulated-causality-first (causal dynamical triangulations)
**Primitive:** causally triangulated spacetimes summed in a path integral. Distinct from Route E: the causal structure is imposed on the triangulation, and de Sitter-like geometry *emerges* (a real result: a 4D extended universe from the sum).
**Strength:** emergent 4D semiclassical spacetime from a causal sum — the best existence proof that causality-first can work non-perturbatively.
**Weakness:** entropy's role is underdeveloped; the continuum limit's physical status is contested; matter is added, not derived.

### Route K — Fixed-point-first (asymptotic safety)
**Primitive:** the renormalization group. Gravity = a non-Gaussian fixed point of the metric's RG flow; entropy/causality/time = not structural inputs.
**Strength:** the most conservative quantum-field-theoretic route — no new principles, just RG taken seriously; evidence for the fixed point is substantial.
**Weakness:** silent on exactly the four vertices' interlock (it is a theory *of* gravity, not of why gravity meets entropy); truncations could deceive.

### Route L — Aeon-first (conformal cyclic cosmology)
**Primitive:** conformal structure across aeons: one universe's conformal infinity *is* the next aeon's big bang. Directly relevant to ETRG-5's stasis cosmology: CCC is the conformal version of "where causality comes to a near stop, a new causality is born" — with the conformal invariance of null rays (Route B's kinematic fact) doing the stitching.
**Strength:** a worked-out cosmological framework with the cone structure as the surviving object across the stasis; testable claims (Hawking points) exist and are contested.
**Weakness:** the entropy issue is its known sore point (the second law across aeons requires gravitational degrees of freedom to dump entropy — the map's own vertex fails to close here); the most criticized leap is that rest mass must fade away at late times for the conformal stitching to exist at all; Hawking-point evidence is disputed.

### Route M — Spectrum-first (noncommutative geometry, the spectral action)
**Primitive:** a spectral triple (algebra, Hilbert space, Dirac operator). Gravity + the *entire Standard Model* from the spectral action; the only route on this map that attempts the matter spectrum — the program's admitted hardest problem (ETRG-1 bold round, Fable's §7).
**Strength:** the Standard Model's gauge group and Higgs *fall out* of geometry (almost-commutative manifolds); if the unification must include the matter spectrum, this is the only existing template.
**Weakness:** entropy and causality are not structural inputs; the predictions' track record is mixed (the Higgs mass miss); the spectral action lives in Euclidean signature — time and causality are *absent*, and Lorentzian NCG is an open repair program. Noted by the referee: F and M are the two halves of Connes' work (modular/thermal time vs spectral geometry) that do not yet talk to each other — and the map's own four-vertex logic says that junction is interesting.

## 4c. The completed terrain, honestly assessed

Thirteen routes. The honest structure that emerges:

- **The four-vertex interlock cluster** (A, B, C, F): the only routes where time, entropy, causality, and gravity are *all* structural. This is the program's home ground, and the meeting points (BW, Jacobson, QNEC) are its foundations.
- **The causality-primitive siblings** (E, H, J): prove the cone-first bet is viable in three independent ways — and none of them has entropy in the load-bearing position the originator's intuition wants. The B/E/H/J cluster is where "causality + something = gravity" is being fought out; B's something is entropy, E's is counting, H's is complex structure, J's is the path-integral measure.
- **The orthodox quantizations** (G, K): gravity quantized, vertices un-unified. They are the program's competition for attention, not for the interlock claim.
- **The cosmological stasis cluster** (L, and ETRG-5): the birth-at-stasis idea, with L its conformal cousin.
- **The spectrum outlier** (M): the only route attacking the matter spectrum — the program's named hardest problem — and the one whose methods (spectral triples) are F's cousins. If the graph ever extends to matter, it extends through M.

No route is complete. Three clusters + two outliers is the honest shape of the terrain as of August 2026.

## 5. Are the routes actually different?

Partially no. Routes A, B, C, F share the same three meeting points and differ mainly in *which vertex is declared primitive* — and "declared primitive" is partly a choice of presentation, not physics. The genuinely different bets are:

- **B vs A:** whether causality is primitive (B) or inherited (A). This is falsifiable in principle: if the cone structure has degrees of freedom the entanglement entropy does *not* fix (beyond the conformal factor), A is incomplete; if every cone deformation is entropy-accounted, B is a rewrite of A. The round-9 domain constraint (the selection principle's power tracks correlation range) is the first data point: it says the entropic account of geometry *degrades* exactly where geometry gets fuzzy — consistent with either, discriminating nothing yet.
- **D vs everything:** holography asserts a boundary theory exists; the others do not. Genuinely different claim, genuinely different universe (AdS).
- **E vs everything:** discreteness as primitive vs emergent. Different claim.

The honest meta-claim: **the unification, if it exists, is probably not a route but the graph** — the four vertices are one structure, and each route is a coordinate chart on it. Charts are individually incomplete; the overlaps (the meeting points) are where the structure is real.

### R1. The map half-inverts the sentence it formalizes

The originator's sentence has a direction: *entropy causes a shift in the spatial orientation of causality* — entropy acts **on the cones**. Route B as stated identifies entropy's kinematic content with Ω, the conformal factor — the one object **light does not read**. So the kinematic half of Route B has entropy living in exactly the sector its own "why light is special" paragraph declares invisible to light. That is an inversion, not a translation.

The sentence's correct formal home is the *dynamical* edge, which the map lists as B's leftover debt ("still needs Jacobson's thermodynamic input") rather than as the intuition itself: entropy variation along null directions sources cone focusing. This is theorem-grade and *directed*: Clausius on Rindler horizons ⇒ Einstein (Jacobson); ANEC from relative-entropy monotonicity (Faulkner–Leigh–Parrikar–Wang 2016); QNEC (Ceyhan–Faulkner 2018). Entropy → cone deformation; light, the pure cone probe, reads the deformation undiluted; matter reads it diluted by the scale. **That is the sentence, verbatim.**

And the repository already proved its sharp form. The Malament split (conformal class × volume element) **is** the unimodular split (trace-free × trace), and the lock note's round-1 theorem — *the entropic input is algebraically the trace-free sector, and light bending reads exactly that sector* — is the statement that entropic stress sources the cone-orienting sector. The "Factor of Two" essay is Route B's dynamical half wearing Route A's clothes. Amended Route B: entropy plays **two roles** — it sets the scale kinematically (Ω; the thermality face A4) and sources the cone sector dynamically (Clausius; the variational face A5) — and the γ = 1 lock is precisely the claim that both roles are one boost generator. The lock note is not a Route-A artifact demoted with its chart; it is the A↔B connective theorem, and it is why the lock sits at the program's center.

### R2. Two circularities the backbone carries (the map flags neither)

1. **The cutoff circularity — the sharp one.** S = A/4Għ read right-to-left fixes Ω only given a UV cutoff: the state-dependent, finite part of entanglement entropy delivers δA (the first law), while the piece proportional to A is cutoff bookkeeping, state-independent. So "entropy supplies the scale" presupposes a scale. Under this pressure Route B either imports the cutoff as an unlisted third primitive or flows into Route E, where counting *is* the cutoff and the measure at once. Add to B's weakness line; it is also why E's parsimony claim in §4 is stronger than the map credits.
2. **Two Ω's.** "Clock rate = Ω" uses the lapse representative (ĝ₀₀ ≡ −1); "S = A/4 fixes Ω" uses the area/volume (unimodular) representative. These are different functionals of the metric, and their identification is Tolman–Ehrenfest — i.e. the A4 face of the lock, valid at entanglement equilibrium with the δ_lock deviation scale, not a free kinematic move. Concretely, "at v → 0 a probe reads Ω alone" holds only in the lapse gauge; in the unimodular gauge the slow probe's Newtonian potential Φ decomposes as 3Φ/2 from the cones minus Φ/2 from the scale. The gauge-free statements are: light reads only the conformal class (theorem); matter reads class *and* Ω (theorem); the 2:1 split is dynamics (Φ = Ψ, PPN γ = 1), not kinematics. The map's "the two contribute equally" quietly assumes the Einstein dynamics it set out to explain.

Two smaller flags: Malament presupposes the manifold, its smooth structure, and distinguishing causality — "the cone field" is not a lone primitive (only Route E buys the manifold too). And *which region's entropy?* Route B answers "causal regions" by fiat, which relocates Route A's hard kernel rather than dissolving it: Q10/P-select is exactly the conjecture that the entropy-selected partition (A) and the causal partition (B) coincide — the A↔B chart-transition function — and round-8's drift_check is its first lattice measurement.
