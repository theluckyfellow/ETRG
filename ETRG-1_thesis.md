# ETRG-1: The Thesis

*Proposed opening frame for the consolidation manuscript. Version 0.1 — July 2026. Amended from the originator's statement by Claude (Fable 5); each clause traces to adjudicated results in this repository (rounds 0–4). Submitted for one final adversarial pass before adoption.*

## One sentence

Time and gravity are the two exchange rates of a single currency — entropy relative to a partition of a timeless quantum universe — locked equal at causal horizons by one generator and one calibration; the doubled bending of light is that lock's fingerprint, and its unitary, laboratory-testable structure is the quantum path to gravity.

## Four clauses

**T1 (Time).** Time — its flow, its rate, and its arrow — is entropy exchange between what an observer tracks and what they do not. A clock is anything that counts that exchange, and nothing finer is available even in principle, because resolving ticks costs entropy. *(A2/A3; Barontini 2026; Erker 2017.)*

**T2 (Gravity: entropy read twice).** Gravity is entropy read twice. Read as rates: entropy exchange runs slower deep in a well, and that gradient of clock rate is time dilation — slow matter falls by maximizing its own aging. That alone is Newton. Read as bookkeeping across horizons: the fine-grained entanglement of the same underlying state writes the curvature of space itself. These are not two mechanisms — they are the thermal face and the variational face of one modular generator under one calibration, so their strengths cannot differ. *(A4/A5; lock note L1–L5; Q10 note N1–N4; theorem-grade at entanglement equilibrium, deviation scale δ_lock = S(ρ‖ρ_vac)/S_BH beyond.)*

**T3 (The fingerprint).** What a particle feels depends on which faces its worldline samples, and the mix grows proportionally with v²: the deflection factor is (1 + v²/c²), running from 1 at rest to exactly 2 at the speed of light. The measured 2:1 ratio is therefore not an anomaly the theory must survive — it is the fingerprint of gravity's entropic origin, because entropy's native home is the null surface, and light is the only probe that reads that surface whole. *(Lock note L2–L4; verified symbolically; the entropic input is algebraically the trace-free sector — Alonso-Serrano & Liška — and lensing reads exactly that sector.)*

**T4 (The quantum path, with teeth).** Because the gravitating entropy is fine-grained and unitary, gravity adds no noise to quantum mechanics — matter-wave interferometry survives, and we bet standing money against gravitational decoherence (P3). Because the two faces are separately measurable in one apparatus, the lock is falsifiable now, in analogue-horizon and cold-atom experiments, with a leg-resolved signature no non-entropic account reproduces (P1, P2 round-3 form). And the one thing entropy cannot write — the overall trace, the cosmological constant — the theory owns as its honest silence: Λ enters as an integration constant, not a prediction. *(A6/A7; P-claims; unimodular clock note U1–U4.)*

## What this thesis does not claim

No new weak-field predictions (the equations are GR's; the claim is structural and explanatory). No derivation of Λ. No resolution of the preferred-factorization problem beyond horizon/diamond observers (Q10 mechanism; cosmological case open). No claim that the constraint algebra closes off-equilibrium in generic non-conformal spacetimes (leading-order CFT case resolved; measure obstruction open). Exact Lorentz invariance of any substrate is assumed, not derived (Q6).
