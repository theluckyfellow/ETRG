# ETRG-6: The Mysteries Map — What the Hypothesis Explains If True, and Where Each Points for Evidence

*August 2026, Kimi-K3, at the originator's strategic question: if the unification of time, entropy, causality, and gravity is true, what else does it explain — and do those angles open new ways to construct evidence? Every entry: the mystery, the explanation the hypothesis offers, the evidence angle it opens, and an honest flag on how strong the link is. ## 1. The equivalence principle (inertial = gravitational mass) — *reinterpretation*

**Mystery:** why does the mass that resists acceleration equal the mass that gravitates?
**Explanation:** mass is the rate of a system's internal modular clock (the Compton frequency — B4), and gravity is the gradient of clock rates (A4). Inertial and gravitational mass are the *same modular rate* read two ways; their equality is the lock, not a coincidence.
**Evidence angle:** atom interferometry tests at improving precision. **Flag (referee-corrected): conditional on B4 (mass = Compton clock rate), a bold-round conjecture nobody has derived — the theorem-grade lock concerns one boost generator at a horizon, not the mass of a lump. And "no deviation at any precision" is also GR's prediction: a bet that can never pay out is not an evidence angle. Reinterpretation, not explanation.**

## 2. The black hole information paradox

**Mystery:** how does information escape evaporation if the entropy is coarse-grained?
**Explanation:** it never was coarse-grained. The two faces merge at the horizon (the lock): the thermodynamic entropy of the horizon *is* fine-grained entanglement, unitary all along; the Page curve is the two books reconciling, not information magically escaping.
**Evidence angle:** the Page curve is, in principle, measurable in an analogue horizon — the entanglement entropy's time evolution. **This is a natural extension of the P2 protocol (a fourth leg). Flag (referee-corrected): the dissolution is a picture, not a mechanism — in gravity, making the fine book turn over took islands/QES; the lock asserts the books agree, it does not derive the turnover. And a *sustained* analogue horizon has no evaporation — there is no turnover to measure; the honest two-face prediction there is the ABSENCE of a Page knee (P2 §9 engineers the knee instead, via reservoir squeeze P-off/P-ramp). The testable content is the bookkeeping claim (P2 row K10), not the word "Page."**

## 3. Dark energy and the smallness of Λ

**Mystery:** why is Λ nonzero but tiny?
**Explanation:** Λ is the universe's tick budget (B7): S_Λ = 3π/ΛG bounds the total entropy exchange the cosmos can register; a universe with a long, complex history requires an enormous budget, hence a tiny Λ. Dark energy is not a substance but the horizon that lets cosmological observers keep time.
**Evidence angle:** the **cosmological lock** — the candidate fourth prediction (ETRG-2, Layer O): H₀, S_Λ, and cosmic clock rates tied through one modular rate. Still unformulated; the numeric consistency check runs now (`cosmic_numbers_check.py`). **Flag: conjecture (B7's adjudicated status: necessity, not sufficiency). Referee's sharpening of the trade: tiny Λ is explained *given* a long complex history as input — the explanandum is traded (why tiny Λ → why a long history), not removed. Still the map's best genuinely new angle.**

## 4. The MOND acceleration scale a₀ ≈ cH₀/2π

**Mystery:** why do galaxy dynamics change character below one particular acceleration, and why is that acceleration the cosmic one?
**Explanation (careful):** the de Sitter horizon's surface gravity *is* cH₀ — the coincidence stops being a coincidence if the cosmic horizon's thermal structure enters the gravitational bookkeeping at low accelerations. This is Verlinde's 2016 claim; the program contains the scale necessarily (the horizon is there), but whether it modifies galaxy dynamics is an *extra* step the program has not derived.
**Evidence angle:** numeric consistency first (the horizon's Unruh acceleration vs measured a₀ — computed in `cosmic_numbers_check.py`); then the hard theory step — does the entropic framework produce an acceleration floor?; then SPARC rotation-curve fits against whatever interpolation the theory supplies (if any). **Flag: the scale coincidence is real and computed; the dynamics link is UNSOLVED and the program claims nothing yet. Verlinde's specific model is under observational pressure (dwarfs, clusters); any program version must face the same data. Referee's addition: the section title picks the a₀ ≈ cH₀/2π convention (ratio 1.15) while the program's own natural quantity — the de Sitter surface gravity κ_dS, the thing whose thermal structure would enter the bookkeeping — misses by 4.5; selecting the convention after seeing the answer is the numerology move the rest of the section avoids. The program currently derives no coefficient; the convention freedom (1, 2π, 1/2π, √Ω_Λ) spans an order of magnitude, and both ratios are of equal standing.**

## 5. The arrow of time and the low-entropy Big Bang

**Mystery:** why did the universe start with entropy low enough for an arrow to exist?
**Explanation:** the arrow is not a boundary condition pasted on — it is what time *is* (coarse-graining); a "beginning" is a stasis point where a new book opens with minimal accumulated entropy (ETRG-5's stasis cosmology: birth-at-stasis comes with a fresh budget by construction).
**Evidence angle:** indirect — stasis phenomenology in Barontini-class systems (A7's exposure) is the nearest testable piece. **Flag: the explanation is elegant and unfalsifiable at present; the stasis mechanics is the only near-term handle.**

## 6. Holographic (metric) noise

**Mystery:** if geometry is entanglement, should spacetime itself fluctuate?
**Explanation:** metric fluctuations inherit entanglement fluctuations (Verlinde–Zurek); the fine-grained vacuum has irreducible entanglement variance, and geometry — being that entanglement — must share it.
**Evidence angle:** ETRG-0's P4, flagged as "the natural next quantitative target" and never sharpened. Sharpening it (an interferometric correlation signature with a predicted spectrum) is an analytic task this program could actually attempt. **Flag: speculative; Holometer-class experiments already constrain some models — a sharpened prediction could be confronted quickly.**

## 7. The Born rule and collapse — *reinterpretation*

**Mystery:** why that measurement rule, and why does collapse *look* irreversible?
**Explanation:** measurement is repartitioning (B8); the Born rule follows from entanglement symmetries (envariance); collapse is the coarse face's bookkeeping of a partition update — irreversible the way any coarse-graining is.
**Evidence angle:** mostly interpretive; envariance's experimental status is foundational-lab territory. **Flag (referee-corrected): envariance is a candidate derivation with a standing contested-circularity literature. An explanation of form, not a new prediction — reinterpretation.**

## 8. The matter spectrum (three generations, the gauge group, the mass ratios)

**Mystery:** why *this* matter?
**Explanation:** the program's honest weakest point — "matter is entanglement debt" classifies debt without predicting denominations. The only route on the graph that attempts the spectrum is M (noncommutative geometry). If the unification is true, the spectrum must live in the partition structure of the vacuum — a problem not yet even toy-formulated.
**Evidence angle:** none near-term. **Flag: open, named the hardest problem since the bold round. No angle yet.**

## 9. Quantum mechanics itself (why complex Hilbert space, why tensor products) — *reinterpretation*

**Mystery:** why does nature use *this* probability calculus?
**Explanation:** the reconstruction theorems (B1) derive the quantum formalism from informational consistency (purification = the two-face principle); QM is what distinguishability bookkeeping must look like.
**Evidence angle:** foundational; tests of the reconstruction axioms (e.g., purification's universality) are quantum-foundations experiments. **Flag: strong as mathematics, thin as new evidence — reinterpretation (same predictions, new vocabulary).**

## The evidence-construction synthesis

Reading the map for *new, constructible* evidence, three angles stand out as actually buildable from here:

1. **The cosmological lock (from §3)** — formulate the H₀–S_Λ–clock-rate relation and check it against measured cosmological numbers. Analytic + numeric; no lab needed. *This is the strongest new angle: it uses measured data that already exists.*
2. **The Page-curve fourth leg of P2 (from §2)** — extend the flagship protocol with the entanglement-entropy time series. Fable (P2's author) is the natural drafter.
3. **The holographic-noise sharpening (from §6)** — turn P4 from a gesture into a spectrum. Analytic; would give interferometer experiments something to aim at.

The MOND angle (§4) is the most seductive and the most dangerous: the scale coincidence is real (computed), but the program currently has *no derived dynamics* at galactic accelerations, and this is exactly where entropic-gravity programs have historically overclaimed. The honest position: compute the coincidence, state the missing step, do not fit rotation curves until the framework supplies the interpolation from inside.
